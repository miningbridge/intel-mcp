# MiningBridge Intelligence

Use the `miningbridge` MCP tools for questions about critical minerals and rare earths. They cover lithium, cobalt, nickel, rare-earth oxides (NdPr), gallium and germanium, spodumene, and battery or base-metal scrap. Use them for trade flows, partner concentration, China exposure, counterparties and research reports, and use the CM-PATRA tools (`get_mineral_patents`, `get_technology_gaps`, `track_rd_project_ip`, `verify_patent_provenance`) for patents and funded R&D on India's critical minerals.

- Call `list_commodities` first if you're unsure what is tracked.
- Commodity arguments accept a name, a partial name or an HS6 code, for example "lithium", "NdPr" or "283691".
- The first call opens a MiningBridge sign-in in the browser. A free account works.
- Present figures as screening magnitudes derived from UN Comtrade, not as price targets or investment advice.
